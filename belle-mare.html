The trip

Ten nights, all of it included

Belle Mare Plage sits on the quiet east coast, away from the busier north. The resort has its own two-kilometre stretch of beach, a lagoon in front of it, and two championship golf courses behind — so it works whether you want to fill the days or do absolutely nothing with them.

All inclusive here means seven restaurants rather than one buffet, so you're not eating the same meal for ten nights. This one is open to everyone — couples, friends, families and solo travellers.

The resort

What's actually on site

07

Restaurants and bars

Seven restaurants across the resort, from beachfront Italian to fine dining, plus a wine cellar with a serious list. All of it included.

  • La Spiaggia — Italian, on the sand
  • La Citronnelle — all-day dining
  • Blue Penny Cellar — wine cellar and tasting room
  • Bars around the pools and beach
10

Nights to fill however you like

Two kilometres of private beach, five swimming pools, a spa, and two 18-hole championship golf courses if you play.

  • U Spa — treatments, sauna, hammam
  • Legend and Links golf courses
  • Watersports on the lagoon
  • Excursions arranged if you want them
