The list

Our destinations

Two of these are open for booking right now. The rest run as group trips — get six of you together and tell us when.

Indian Ocean

Mauritius

The one people come back for. Beach on one side, sugar cane and mountains on the other, and resorts that genuinely earn their five stars. Half board or all inclusive, long lazy days, and enough to do that nobody gets restless — catamarans, the botanical gardens, the south coast, Port Louis market.

Open now: Constance Belle Mare Plage, 20–30 September, 10 nights all inclusive, £2,785 per person sharing.

United Arab Emirates

Dubai & Abu Dhabi

Two cities that feel nothing like each other, an hour and a half apart. Abu Dhabi for the quiet end — Saadiyat Island, the beach, the Louvre, the Grand Mosque. Dubai for everything else: the Mall, the Marina, the desert, the old creek and the gold souk. Doing both in one week is the way to see them properly.

Open now: Saadiyat Island & Dubai, 15–22 September, 7 nights, £950 per person sharing — or £850 each if six or more of you travel together.

CityBeachDesert7 nights

Qatar

Doha

Underrated, and small enough to actually see in a few days. The Museum of Islamic Art, the Corniche at dusk, Souq Waqif in the evening, and the inland sea out in the dunes. Works beautifully as a short break on its own or as a few nights bolted onto a longer trip east.

CityCultureShort breakStopover

Indonesia

Bali

Two halves to a good Bali trip: a few nights inland at Ubud among the rice terraces, temples and the forest, then down to the coast for the beach and the sunsets. Private pool villas cost far less than the equivalent anywhere else, which is why groups do so well here.

VillasBeachCulture10–14 nights

Thailand

Phuket

The base for the whole Andaman coast. Beach clubs and five-star resorts on the west side, and a boat out to Phang Nga Bay, the Phi Phi islands or Similan whenever you fancy it. Easy to combine with a few nights in Bangkok on the way through.

IslandsBeachBoat daysCombine with Bangkok

Malaysia

Malaysia

Kuala Lumpur to start — the towers, the food, the night markets — then out to Langkawi or Penang for the island half. Straightforward for Muslim travellers, halal food everywhere without having to ask, and very good value for what you get.

Saudi Arabia

Umrah packages

Fully guided, from the moment you land. Hotels, transport between Makkah and Madinah, and someone with the group throughout who knows what they are doing. We keep these groups small on purpose — it makes a real difference to how the days go. Tell us your dates and how many of you and we will come back with the package and the price.

Türkiye

Antalya

Where our ultra all-inclusive trips go, and the reason people ask for the next date before the last one has landed. Everything is covered once you are through the door, the old city is worth a day on its own, and the flight is short enough that a four-night trip is genuinely worth doing.
