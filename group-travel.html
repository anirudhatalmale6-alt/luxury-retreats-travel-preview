Who travels as a group

Bring your own group, or join ours

A group can be whatever you want it to be. A group of ladies who would rather travel with women. A group of friends who have been talking about it for years. Two or three families going away together. All of them travel with us on the same basis.

The one requirement for the exclusive group rate is six people travelling together. Get to six and the price drops, the rooms are held as one block, and the whole trip is put together around your group rather than around a calendar.

Groups stay small on purpose — never more than twenty — because that is the difference between a holiday and a coach tour. If you are travelling on your own, you can still join one of our open departures below and arrive to a group that is already talking.

Before you ask

The questions we get most

How many of us do we need?

Six travelling together is the minimum for the exclusive group rate. Under six you are very welcome on any of our open departures at the normal price.

Do I have to share a room?

No. Twin-share keeps the cost down and we'll match you with someone, but a single room is always an option for a supplement.

Can it be ladies only?

Yes. Tell us when you enquire and the group and the rooming are women only throughout. It is one of the options, not the only one.

What does the deposit actually do?

It holds your place. The rest is due before departure and you can pay it in one go or spread it — your booking page shows what's left.

Is the food halal?

It is arranged with the resort before we arrive on any trip where you ask for it. Any other dietary needs, tell us when you book.

Are flights included?

On most trips, no — it is stated on every one. We will tell you which flights the group is on so you can book alongside.
