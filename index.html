How booking works

The part most people ask about. You do not pay for the whole holiday on day one.

Pay your deposit

Pick your trip and pay the deposit by card. Your place is held from that moment, and you get a confirmation straight away.

Pay the balance your way

Pay it off whenever suits — in one go, or in chunks — through your own booking page. We send a reminder before the due date.

We do the rest

Rooms, transfers, excursions and the group chat. You get an itinerary and a person to call if anything changes.

Who we are

Travel that feels looked after

Luxury Retreats Travel is a small UK operation putting together escapes to places worth the flight — Mauritius, the UAE, Qatar, Bali, Phuket, Malaysia, Antalya, and Umrah packages.

We keep the groups small, stay in the resorts we sell, and answer the phone ourselves. A lot of what we run is women travelling together, which is where the Ladies Only Group came from.
