Why it exists

Travelling on your own, but not alone

Plenty of women want to see the world and haven't got anyone to go with — or would rather not be the only woman in a mixed group. That is the whole reason this exists.

You book on your own and arrive to a group that is already talking. There is a chat before the trip, so by the time you land you know who everyone is. Rooms are twin-share if you want to keep the cost down, and single if you would rather have your own space.

The groups are small on purpose — never more than twenty — because that is the difference between a holiday and a coach tour.

Before you ask

The questions we get most

I'd be coming on my own

Most people are. There's a group chat before the trip, so you land already knowing names, and you're hosted the whole way.

Do I have to share a room?

No. Twin-share keeps the cost down and we'll match you with someone, but a single room is always an option for a supplement.

Is the food halal?

On the sisters' trips, yes, and it's arranged with the resort before we arrive. Any other dietary needs, tell us when you book.

What does the deposit actually do?

It holds your place. The rest is due before departure and you can pay it in one go or spread it — your booking page shows what's left.

Are flights included?

On most trips, no — it's stated on every one. We'll tell you which flights the group is on so you can book alongside.

How big is the group?

Never more than twenty, usually a good deal less. Big enough for company, small enough to actually get to know people.
