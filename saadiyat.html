The trip

One week, two worlds

Saadiyat Island is where Abu Dhabi keeps its quiet: white sand, clear water, turtles nesting on a protected beach, and resorts that face nothing but the sea. Dubai is the opposite, and that is the point of doing both in one week.

You start slow on the beach and finish in the middle of everything, without the faff of arranging two separate holidays.

Where you're staying

Two hotels, one week

Both five-star, and both picked for where they put you — one on the sand, one in the middle of Dubai.

Nights 1 – 3 · Abu Dhabi

Park Hyatt Abu Dhabi Hotel & Villas

On Saadiyat Beach itself, facing nine kilometres of protected natural coastline. Low-rise, quiet, and about as far from a city hotel as you can get while still being twenty minutes from Abu Dhabi.

  • Directly on Saadiyat Beach
  • Four pools and a beach club
  • Atarmia Spa on site
  • Louvre Abu Dhabi ten minutes away

The photographs above are of Saadiyat Island itself. We'll put the Park Hyatt's own room and pool photography here as soon as the hotel sends the gallery over.

Nights 4 – 7 · Dubai

Swissôtel Al Murooj Dubai

Right in Downtown Dubai with the Burj Khalifa behind it and Dubai Mall across the road — so the fountains, the shopping and the restaurants are a walk away, not a taxi ride.

  • Opposite Dubai Mall & Burj Khalifa
  • Lagoon-style pools and gardens
  • Rooms and suites with city views
  • Several restaurants on site
